Data source and statistic: NHANES (first to last cycle), US adults aged 20+, survey weighted obesity prevalence (BMI ≥ 30kg/m^2 ) with 95% confidence intervals

Exclusions: Participants below age 20, those with missing BMI or missing income group values.

Limitation: data cuts across different populations/demographics in terms of age, therefore differences in income group should be noted as associations and not causal links.
